## 1. App Idea & Purpose
The **Amazon Shopping App** is a mobile-focused web shopping experience that helps customers discover products, compare options, save items for later, place orders, and track deliveries in one convenient, clear, and reliable place.

- **Target Users:** Online shoppers who want to find products, compare choices, complete purchases, and monitor deliveries.
- **Problem Solved:** Customers need a single, straightforward place to discover products, understand their features and prices, complete purchases, and know when their orders will arrive.
